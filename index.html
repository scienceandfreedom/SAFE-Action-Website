    How it works

    STEP 01

    We read everything

    Our tracker ingests every bill filed in 50 states and Congress and flags the ones that touch vaccines, public health, research funding, and science education.

    STEP 02

    You see it move

    Every tracked bill shows its status, last action, and a plain-English summary of what it does, updated daily as hearings, floor votes, and signatures are recorded.

    STEP 03

    Your rep hears from you

    One click opens a prewritten, editable message to the exact lawmakers who will vote on it. Two minutes, counted, and on the record.
