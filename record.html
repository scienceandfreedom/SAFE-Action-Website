METHODOLOGY

This record lists sponsorships, cosponsorships, and recorded votes on bills tracked by SAFE Action, drawn from official legislative sources. It is a record of legislative activity, not a rating of any person. It does not endorse or oppose anyone. Classifications apply to bills, not people.
