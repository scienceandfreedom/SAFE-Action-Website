Legislative Records

A record of which legislators sponsored, cosponsored, or cast recorded votes on the bills SAFE Action tracks. Each entry links to the official bill record and shows the date of the most recent recorded action. Filter by state, level, chamber, party, or topic, or search by name or bill.

METHODOLOGY

This record lists sponsorships, cosponsorships, and recorded votes on bills tracked by SAFE Action, drawn from official legislative sources. It is a record of legislative activity, not a rating of any person. It does not endorse or oppose anyone. Classifications apply to bills, not people. Votes are recorded roll-call votes on tracked bills, as published by the legislature; absence of a vote means no recorded roll call, not a position.

DEVELOPMENT FIXTURE

The entries shown are placeholder test data. Every name is fictional. This is not a real legislative record.
