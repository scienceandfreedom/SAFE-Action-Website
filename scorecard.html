Science Scorecard

Who is introducing, cosponsoring, and voting on the bills we track. Counts of official acts on legislation SAFE Action classifies as pro-science or anti-science, with every number linked to the record behind it.

This scorecard is a tally of legislative acts, not a rating of any person. It does not endorse or oppose anyone. It lists every legislator in the database and is refreshed nightly from official legislative sources.
